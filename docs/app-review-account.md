## Reviewnotities (kopiëren naar App Store Connect / Play Console)

> Pinacot is a personal diary for artworks you've seen in museums. Log in
> with the account above — it already contains a few marked-as-seen
> artworks so the core flow is visible immediately.
>
> Core flow to test:
> 1. Go to "Kunstenaars" (Artists) and open any artist.
> 2. Open an artwork and tap "Markeer als gezien" (Mark as seen) to log a
>    visit with an optional photo, date, location, rating and note.
> 3. "Profiel" (Profile) shows the collected artworks and lets you export
>    your data or delete the account.
>
> No in-app purchases or subscriptions are active in this build.
